Shark of the Month · October 2026

Goblin Shark

Mitsukurina owstoni · Deep sea · Not a danger to people

Length 3-4 mDepth 200-1,300 mConservation: Least Concern
👹HERO ILLUSTRATION

The weird bit

Its jaws fire forward at up to 3 m/s to snatch prey, a trick called slingjaw feeding. Nothing else in the shark world does it quite like this.

Where it lives

Deep waters of the Atlantic, Pacific and Indian oceans. It is rarely seen alive.

Three facts

1. Its skin looks pink because blood vessels show through.
2. Its lineage goes back about 125 million years.
3. Needle-like teeth at the front, crushing teeth at the back.
