Myth vs fact

Sharks must keep swimming or they drown.

Partly true. Great whites and makos do. Nurse sharks can rest on the sea floor and pump water over their gills. Source: Smithsonian
Records

Oldest vertebrate

The Greenland shark may live 400+ years. Source: Science, 2016
Senses

Electric sixth sense

Sharks detect tiny electric fields with jelly-filled pores called ampullae of Lorenzini.
History

Older than trees

Sharks have been around for over 450 million years; the first trees showed up about 385 million years ago.
